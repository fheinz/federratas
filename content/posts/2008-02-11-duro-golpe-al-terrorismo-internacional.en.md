date: 2008-02-11

*Madrid, Barajas, from our correspondent.*

Spanish border police today thwarted, with their usual efficiency and
celerity, a terrorist attack even before the conspirators could set
their plan in motion.  During routine carry-on luggage inspection
operations, they seized a can of southern Pacific abalone (shellfish
commonly known as "locos"), which a suspicious-looking individual was
attempting to smuggle aboard a flight to Italy.

"The logistics of these attacks are becoming increasingly devilishly
intricate," a border police spokesperson confirmed to this outlet. "In
this case, the can, which the miscreant purchased at a duty-free shop
in the pre-boarding area of the Santiago airport from Chile, was
brought aboard an Iberia flight. We suspect is that he was working
with two other accomplices: one of them would attempt to smuggle a can
opener on board, while the other would do the same with a container of
mayonnaise. With these elements, it would have been very simple for
them to threaten the pilot with a serious liver attack unless he
handed over control of the plane, with the aim of crashing it onto
King Juan Carlos's bald spot."

Fortunately, the Royal Bald Spot is safe thanks to the gallant actions
of the security forces, who with nobility remind us that the only way
they can think of in order to improve security involves the pointless
harassment of passengers.
