title: "Love at First Sight"
date: 2006-07-11
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Ellas", "Paren el mundo"]

The thing is they never accepted him from the beginning it was
jealousy anger envy lack of empathy that I'm so much in love so happy
to have found him suddenly coming into my life when neither me nor
they expected it so that's why they try to dissuade me there's no love
at first sight the old shrews tell me you'll get over it think it over
reconsider come back come back they shout at me through loudspeakers
while I hold on to him and press my body against his back and I am
full of him of his smell of his manly body <!--more--> of his raspy
voice that defies the world the thunder the lights I frantically cling
to his leather jacket in the rain that baptizes us and to think they
call themselves my friends and they can't be happy for the fact that I
love a real man that I choose this adventure instead of the tedium of
loneliness or a husband that's why they want to pull me away from him
take me back to a life without love just as I've found him the same
day he arrived and saw me and chose me for himself out of everyone
else yes also out of them and that's what they can't stand and because
it's me and not them that's why they want to call me back remind me of
the newspaper clippings the photographs of mutilated bodies the police
sketches the horrified tales of chance survivors the walls pitted by
shotgun blasts as if I were stupid as if I didn't know as if I cared
as if any of them had talked about anything else in the last two weeks
as if there really was anything more important than him and what I am
through him as if the world had existed before he burst like that into
the bank's lobby shouting and brandishing his shotgun and our eyes met
and I understood that it was me he was shouting for that mine were the
shots his weapon ejaculated mine the lives he reaped with them that it
was him and only him that mattered more than money more than the Law
more than freedom and life and the dead and love itself and they know
it and they keep quiet they keep quiet so they can say the other
things so they can pity me accuse me from their disgusting pose of
respectable ladies that keeps them distracted from the shame of
knowing themselves rejected from the irreversible reality that here
now for him forever the only hostage is me it's me me me and I'm not
going to let them take him from me I'm not going to endure the
jealousy of them taking him away to give him to the police to the
judges to the prison together they'll have to take us together because
they won't be able to pry us apart so tight is my embrace that I am
already him we are already one when I take the knife from his belt and
drive it hard into his belly and pull the handle tearing skin muscles
guts and he melts he spills slowly in my hands hot he flows soft
liquid inert mine more mine than ever more mine than anyone eternally
mine mine and no one else's.
