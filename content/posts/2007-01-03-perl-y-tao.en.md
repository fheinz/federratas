Master E-Squizo learned that other hackers were tormenting Perlhead
for his choice of programming language. E-Squizo summoned them one
evening under the cherry tree and spoke to them thusly:

> Each programming language corresponds to a part of the
> hacker. Smalltalk, for example, corresponds to the soul, for it is
> pristine, pure, orthogonal to the point of lacking flow
> control. Just as Pascal and Modula-2 belong to the left hemisphere
> of the brain, talkative and structured, Lisp belongs to the right
> hemisphere: creative, intuitive, complex, twisted. C and Java
> correspond to the stomach, as they provide our sustenance. C++ is
> reflected in the large intestine: everyone knows it is there, and
> that it serves some function, but no one likes to think about
> it. COBOL corresponds, evidently, to the colon. Python is for the
> liver, capable of metabolizing massive amounts of syntactic sugar,
> and Perl corresponds to sex: writing programms in it, the hacker
> feels at once filthy and powerful, filled with an intense, guilty
> pleasure.

At that moment, E-Squizo's disciples were enlightened, and they spent
the next few months writing variants of "Just another Perl hacker,"
each in their favorite programming language.
