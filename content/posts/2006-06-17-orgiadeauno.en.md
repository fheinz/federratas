On 8 February 1994, UK newspapers reported [the untimely death of
Mr.  Stephen
Milligan](http://news.bbc.co.uk/onthisday/hi/dates/stories/february/8/newsid_2538000/2538165.stm),
Honorable Member of the British Parliament for the Conservative Party.

The day before, Mr Milligan had been found on the kitchen table of his
home. The only garments he wore at the time of his demise by
asphyxiation were a pair of sheer silk stockings and a matching garter
belt. His head was wrapped in a polyethylene bag, tightened at the
neck with an electrical cord, and he had a peeled orange in his mouth.

May the many and cherished practitioners of onanism rest assured that
no words of mine should ever be read as a slight toward the noble and
millennial art of orgasmic self-procurement. However, it's evident
that what Milligan was doing at that moment went far beyond simple
masturbation, leaving even sophisticated variants like the "luxury
wank" (with a condom), the "umbrella," and even the "sleeper" far
behind.

Which lesser label than "one-person orgy" could do justice to this
solitary sexual act, planned and executed with meticulous dedication,
in which a subject commits to pleasure and his own way of obtaining it
to such an extreme that he won't allow anything to distract him, not
even Death herself? What happens to the airs of superiority of the
orgiast orthodoxy, many of them even liable to interrupt a pleasurable
shag to answer the telephone?

The one-person orgy particularly well-suited to people seeking
certainty. It's the easiest one to organize, the most hygienic, the
one where undesirables never sneak in, the only one where perfect
attendance is guaranteed. In it, there is not the slightest
possibility of unforeseen pregnancies, or of a surprise encounter with
one's spouse. Those who pay attention to such minutiae may argue that
every one-person orgy is, by necessity, strictly homosexual, but who
cares?

Unlike the churrigueresque mass orgies in which abundance and excess
may be used to mask any shortcoming, the one-person orgy is the
quintessential orgy, the one from which nothing can be removed without
depriving it of its nature.

And deep down, beyond what appearances might suggest, a good orgy is
always an individual one. Whoever doesn't understand this ignores the
fact that, among all experiences, ecstasy and death are the loneliest.
