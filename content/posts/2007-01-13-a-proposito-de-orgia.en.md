---
title: "On the Subject of “Orgies”"
date: 2007-01-13
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Orgías"]
---

The word “orgy” comes to us straight and almost non-stop from the
Greek, or maybe Latin; it hardly matters at this point.

It's remarkable for the meaning and even the spelling of such an old
word to have changed so little over time. We can in fact infer a lot
about the disposition of the people of that era just by observing that
they reserved, right from the outset, a two-syllable word to name a
concept that today would require us (if we wished to avoid it) to
resort to much longer monstrosities like “having a blast,” “painting
the town red” (oh, the euphemisms!), or “raising hell.”

This difference in how easy it is to express the same concept suggests
that, in those times, the term was used much more frequently than it
is now, and therefore demanded a convenient and short utterance to
convey it. This is an excellent moment to be very, very jealous of
them.

While there is a great variety of them, and many nuances within each
variety, the defining elements of a self-respecting orgy are the
crowd, the lack of control, and promiscuity.

Of these aspects, perhaps the one that has undergone the most curious
development throughout history is that of the crowd. Contrary to what
logic might suggest, as the world's population increased, the number
of people required to qualify as an orgiastic "crowd" has steadily
decreased. This has reached such an extreme that three lonely people
together in a bed are now celebrated with orgy fanfares, whereas in
better times they would have barely merited a curt and everyday “my
sister-in-law was in town” as a description.

Another feature that deserves our attention is the lack of control. An
alien discovering how pleasant and liberating the experience of
abandon is for our species would conclude, without a doubt, that it's
our absolutely favorite state. They would assume the only way to
prevent a human being from letting going out of control at all hours
is by whipping them while chained hand and foot. Surprisingly, the
difficult thing regarding control these days is, precisely, letting go
of it. From which we may deduce we have already been chained and
whipped, but that's a subject for another day.

This weird tension has been the breeding ground for a vast industrial
complex that extracts fat profits from the the brewing, synthesis,
cultivation and commercialization of both legal and illegal substances
intended to relax the conflict between our innate orgiastic vocation
and our cultural reluctance to give in to pleasure. Once again, we
learn that repression is the mother of prosperity.

We are left with the final, defining requirement of the orgy, the one
that has been the subject of the worst press of all: promiscuity, more
specifically sexual promiscuity.

Let us try and avoid, however difficult it may be, the hateful
comparison between the primal musicality of the “orgy” and the modern,
guilt-inducing cacophony of “promiscuity.”  Let us concentrate,
instead, on what this word describes in this precise context: a pile
of human bodies that, as if obeying orders emanating directly from
Oliverio Girondo move, draw close, graze, pile up, rub, lick,
lubricate, caress, penetrate, kiss, sink, rise, enjoy, moan, gasp,
laugh&hellip;

With all due respect for whatever opinion readers may have on these
matters, I'll take the liberty of stating for the record that it
sounds to me like a much, much better better time investment than a
Sunday afternoon watching football on the telly.
