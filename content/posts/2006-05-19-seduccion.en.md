---
title: "Seduction"
date: 2006-05-19
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Ellas", "Paren el mundo"]
---

A dozen perfect red roses please red roses oozing dew and desire of
course sir that will be twenty bucks how about adding a card no no
thanks well here they are I leave the flower shop for her house
running skipping long strides my incantations at the ready to ward off
demons lurking along the way for days I move at a forced march through
swamps mountains rivers dragon nests fields aflame the roses held high
always red always moist <!--more--> they shine they illuminate the way they
penetrate the fog they guide my steps to her threshold I knock the
door and the roses burst in flames when she arrives all jade
sandalwood snow and mahogany blinded by the light I give her the roses
and when she takes them they are a bunch of serpents wrapped in
cellophane she screams she faints I scream too I catch the snakes I
run back to the flower shop leaving her unconscious this time the path
is downhill clean snow the vipers freeze they turn into skis I put
them on and glide on them the cold wind on my face I reach the shop I
go in shouting why did you sell me these roses that are serpents and
in my hands again perfect moist red roses it's because those are roses
that I want you for muyself they sell a lot and I thought that you
might but well if that's not the case I'll exchange them for let's see
what you like I have carnations that I no longer love you orchids that
give me your sex tulips that no no no none of that I simply want
luminous moist red roses roses that are roses are roses are roses and
nothing more as you wish sir and don't worry they're much cheaper
you'll get many many more than your dozen let me send them you can't
carry them yourself I go out again with the roses on their way I run
back to her house I cross valleys dodge forests defy storms climb
mountains and at last her house is no longer there it disappeared
under an ocean of red roses an endless sea from horizon to horizon
made of petals and thorns and cards that say "I love you" they rise
and fall in waves that break and lick the stone coast she must still
be in her flower-flooded house I run to the beach I plunge my feet up
to my knees in the cutting thorny tide I dive headfirst and swim with
all my might toward the place where her house should be but I never
make it first my clothes then my skin my flesh my bones are torn apart
they spill they dissolve they merge with thorns stems petals and I
begin to slowly drip drip drip towards the bottom towards the place
where her blushing blood is eagerly awaiting my arrival.
