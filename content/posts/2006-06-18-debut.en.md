The auditorium is loud with voices posh people sequins silk ties fine
stockings identical suits motionless manes and an iron will to hear
see applaud everyone and they don't know it yet but also me because
I'm going to read my story and I'm happy because I like my story it's
about love and it has laughter and there's a crystal blue lake and at
the end she jumps from the balcony and falls forever and keeps falling
falling falling <!--more--> and I get more and more nervous because
the curtain opens and the opening act is a musical version of *The
Metamorphosis* for some reason all the actors are dressed as Richard
Nixon and the actresses as Groucho Marx and now I realize it's a
theater showcase and not a literary one so I try to escape sneak out
under the seats and behind the doors and through the forest of the
audience's legs until I recognize the ankles of my primary school
teacher who leans down and tells me don't be a fool there are few
artists and they let everyone through get naked and go up on stage
they're going to like you so I take off my clothes right there in the
foyer and I hide to review the text in front of a window behind the
heavy red velvet curtains that stick to my skin and hug me and squeeze
me and wet me and lick me while I struggle to unfold the story that's
written on the paper I used to wrap the ham and tomato sandwich
finally I open it but the text is no longer on the sheet it's on the
sandwich a page and a half of words printed on the pieces of brown
bread I practice how to leaf through a sandwich without the bread
falling apart or the tomato falling out or the curtains getting
stained with mayo and ketchup and above all without the reading the
pornographic ham and I might end up fucking it all up I practice and
practice but I'm still surprised when the curtains open and they're
now on the stage instead of in front of the window which is no longer
there and I'm in the middle of the stage dressed in a green tutu that
someone must have put on me without me noticing and people who look at
me and look at me and look at me in deep silence as if waiting for me
to give them permission and when I look down I see the story and the
sandwich and the ham and I cannot repress the voracious hunger the
ferocious hunger the mortal hunger I bite I devour nouns I choke on
adverbs and prepositions I'm sure the adjectives will give me
indigestion I tear verbs apart and conjugate them gastrically until
I'm done and I'm left there on stage my hands bloodied with tomato and
mayo covering my face and discovering that I don't remember the words
or the story or the name of the main character or even if there was a
main character but yes it was about love and I can no longer speak and
I'm ashamed I blush I loathe myself I faint and I fall I fall slowly
senseless into the infinite darkness of the floorboards through air
that's become dense soft viscous with applause and cheers.
