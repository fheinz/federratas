---
title: "Contest"
date: 2006-11-10
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Paren el mundo"]
---

It's night in my room but it's not my room because I never had a room
like this and much less one that was missing an entire wall and where
the window looked out directly onto the cliff where the waves break
and roar and splash so high they look like they're going to come
through the window but in the end it's only a tiny drizzle that comes
through like a cold salty mist that suddenly realizes it has no
business inside my room and goes back out the way it came <!--more-->
so I don't get angry and next to the window I have the professional
electric super-flexible four-cylinder fishing rod and I put bait on
the hook and cast the line out the window and immediately I pull up a
huge shiny silvery fish that jumps and twists and I'm happy because
I'm going to win the contest but the judge comes in and looks at my
fish and says this hook is not regulation be very careful with what
you do here this is the hook you have to use take it and gives me a
tea kettle I tear off the forbidden hook and tie the kettle to the end
of the line and I put the bait inside it and I cast it out the window
again and right then a swordfish bites much bigger than the first one
and it's a rainbow of colors and much heavier although it's not hard
for me to lift it and the judge comes and scolds me again and tells me
he can't have any more patience with me I shouldn't keep using
non-regulation hooks or he'll disqualify me and gives me a TV antenna
I don't know which bait you use with an antenna but from the darkness
outside the room my Mum appears and tells me the best thing are the
frozen oysters that are on the rock next to the nightstand on the side
where the wall is missing I grab them and while I carry them to the
window they thaw and open and tongues tentacles tender warm velvety
flesh come out they suck my fingers so I put them in a shoebox and I
haven't even managed to turn around before one has already climbed on
top of the other and I'm amazed and get moved and think look at that
the force of nature the critters just thawed a few seconds ago and
they're already shagging and to think I wanted to kill them but then I
realize that the one on top is actually eating the one on the bottom
and I get angry because it's going to leave me without any bait so I
run to look for the rod before it's too late and I can't find it
anywhere instead I grab a hammer which works just as well and when I
go back to the shoebox it turns out it's no longer inside it's escaped
and it looks like that after eating its companion it also climbed onto
a stool and ate the cold chicken I was keeping as a snack and went
into hiding I'm looking all over the room searching in the closets the
shelves between the pages of books in the jars and under the boat
being careful it doesn't bite me in the end it's under the bed wrapped
in a towel in the teddy bear's hug and I take the hammer and fire once
twice three times the bullets scream bang bang bang bang but just then
a tide of carpet rises and the oyster sinks into the parquet digging
digging digging with its tongue until nothing is left above floor
level.
