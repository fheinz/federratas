---
title: "The Treat"
date: 2007-03-01
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Cuentos Chinos"]
---

For many years, pheasants had been absent from the Emperor's
gardens. The sovereign had sent emissaries to the nine corners of the
world in search of them, and all had returned empty-handed. Until one
day, while the monarch rested from his many occupations contemplating
the dance of the waters in his favorite fountain, a beautiful pheasant
stepped out from behind the bushes and stopped to drink and show off
its plumage.

The Emperor ordered a great feast to celebrate the find. The nobles
came with their wives and mistresses. Dignitaries from remote lands
attended in splendorous finery to present their letters of
congratulation, and the people danced in the streets. It was two weeks
of festivities featuring musicians, dancers, jugglers, acrobats,
magicians, and clairvoyants.  Days in which everyone regaled
themselves with milk, honey, liquor, and sweets, culminating in a
grand banquet under the light of fireworks, where everyone enjoyed the
most exquisite dishes.

But only the Emperor ate pheasant.
