---
title: "Difference"
date: 2007-03-17
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Cuentos Chinos"]
---

The Tiger caught the Fox. He was getting ready to kill him when the
Fox said: "Don't you dare hurt me! The Emperor of Heaven has made me
King of All Beasts, and all fear me, as you should too. See for
yourself: take me where the other animals are, and you will see them
all flee from my presence."

Intrigued, the Tiger grabbed the Fox by the neck and led him through
the forest. Upon seeing the Tiger, the animals invariably fled in
terror.

— See? — said the clever Fox — The mere sight of me strikes fear into
their hearts!

Impressed, the Tiger took the Fox to a secluded part of the jungle and
proceeded to devour him before going on his way. After a while, he
reached the clearing where the Chickens were waiting for him.

— Is it done? — they asked anxiously.

— Yes. — replied the Tiger — But you told me it was just a Fox, and
it turned out to be the King of All Beasts. We need to talk about the
price again.
