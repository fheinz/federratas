---
title: "The Motive of God"
date: 2006-06-25
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Paren el mundo"]
---

Saturday morning downtown forty degrees heavy sunlight crushing me
against the sidewalk perspiration moisture and people so many people
from here to there they run they walk they keep me from finding you
and why can't they stay still for a while how do they manage to avoid
bumping into each other they walk by me with their unfamiliar faces
people cross my path brush against me look at me say excuse me or
sorry and we share a moment and yet I still don't know anything about
this guy except that on Saturday he was sucking heat in the city but I
don't know about his life <!--more--> whether he has a job and what
job whether he’s married whether he loves her whether he has children
what happens to him when I don't see him maybe he doesn't even exist
and he’s only there to say sorry to me and continue the journey and
disappear forever behind the next corner unless I follow him and spy
on him and then he continues to exist and as I find out more about him
the undefined man and his average face become real distinguishable
unique like yours and he ends up having a job and a boss who bullies
him and a past in a secondary school and a first girlfriend that he
barely remembers and maybe by then it's too late and from then on the
poor guy has no choice but to exist even when I’m not looking no no no
too much responsibility better I don’t follow him better I keep
looking for you in the middle of the crowd of lawyers florists
children with sniffles women in miniskirts the gun in my pocket
weighing me down why so many people why you if I was enough because
when it comes down to it I am the one who sees them and I am the one
who’s looking for you it was enough with me as the only infinite
eternal imagineer of wonders explorer of my mind inventor of
sensations dreamer of everything although of course eternity is a long
time and there comes a point where I can't think of anything new
anymore but since I liked it I imagine everything again and again and
again until the permutations are exhausted all repetitions and
amazement are gone and out of boredom I end up creating something
anything a stone an algebra a wind I don’t know something different
something out there something outside of me and I get drunk in its
strangeness in the novelty of something that’s not me and I get
excited creating things places sensations monsters hells until again
everything is created all the times in every way and I get stuck again
wanting something more but there is nothing more because my creations
are all there is and me who was always the same all the time and I
can't stand myself anymore I want to be someone else I want to get
away see myself from the outside not knowing not understanding so I
invent an amusement park full of others billions of lives scattered
throughout the worlds for centuries I fill it with hunters kings
mothers witches lovers shepherds chess players ballerinas madmen
traitors beggars astronauts Buddhists athletes secretaries perverts
anybody and everybody the important thing is the contradiction the
contrast the variety each life is different first a lascivious
Cleopatra then a happy child who in adulthood dies of leprosy then a
movie star now a murderer who’s looking for you then it will be you
fleeing from my revenge and then the judge who condemns me and the
executioner who kills me all my lives all my deaths each one of the
people who sweat and stumble on the pedestrian street it is me who
pushes me I am the one who looks at me without recognizing myself
without realizing it even though the hints are there in all religions
reincarnation love your neighbor as yourself Muhammad and the Prophet
the Father the Son and the Holy Spirit the cycle of Shiva eternal life
yin and yang and what do I do now that I know that the universe is my
creation that the universe is I why live without pain vertigo laughter
fear how can I hate you now that I know you're my accomplice the gun
burns in my pocket I take it out I shoot around me there go two three
heads that dissolve into crimson tulips I aim at my temple and bang
the floor undulates under my body turns red and so does the sky and
the people and the girl the girl the girl from whom I scream the
horror of my liberation.
