---
title: "And how about we aaall go out dancing…"
date: 2006-07-28
categories: ["Atentados paraliterarios", "Ellas"]
---

Versatility and the lack of biological specialization are,
undoubtedly, essential characteristics of our species. While a worker
termite can never serve its queen as a soldier, and there is only
indirect and contradictory evidence of the existence of the Watchmaker
Monkey, the human being's ability to fulfill multiple roles has been
very useful in ensuring that we are the ones eating the animals, and
not the other way around.

Surely this must be the origin of women's innate tendency to demand
various and conflicting virtues from their suitors, to the point where
we come to envy animals for the simplicity of their courtship
rituals. The peacock, for example, who just needs to unfold his fan of
feathers, or the praying mantis himself, from whom nothing more is
expected than to allow the female to devour his head during
copulation. In our wretched species, however, the male must satisfy a
stricter list of virtues to conquer a lady's heart than to perform
surgery on it.

There's no doubt this state of affairs responds to Mother Nature's
wisdom, whispering into the ear of the fertile sex what is important
for the survival of the species in each environment. But is there any
explanation for why a man is supposed to know how to dance? What
evolutionary advantage is conferred by the ridiculous tendency to
waste energy on clearly superfluous movements? Is there anyone whose
stomach does not churn at the mere thought of Humphrey Bogart dancing
the Lambada?

Those who know me will argue that my aversion to dancing is due to the
fact that the best that can be said about my skill as a dancer is that
I rarely trip over my own feet more than two or three times a
night. But there are multiple reasons why, in the ranking of pleasure,
most men place a night of dance just behind a root canal.

Scientific studies have shown that nightclubs operated only in those
places already identified as absolutely unsuitable for any purpose
whatsoever, including dance. This still works due to the fact that the
owners of these Dantean anterooms know that people go there to dance,
and not to have a good time. Thus, no one seems to care about the
humiliation of having a bodybuilder with difficulties writing his own
last name without spelling errors decide whether one can enter or not.
Nor the fact that in nightclubs one sees nothing, hears nothing, and
the only thing preventing us from rolling on the ground, our bodies
shattered by elbow strikes, is the pile of passed-out drunks that
already cover it wall-to-wall. And to add infamy to what is already
offensive, they charge us more than women, who are the ones who
actually want to go!

All that, however, is nothing compared to the greatest oprobium of
all: the guys who *do* know how to dance. These abominable gender
traitors do not only exist, some of them are not even gay. Our fickle
companions, for their part, need only set foot on the dance floor to
forget everything about our distinguished lineage, our gallant figure
and our dazzling credentials to shamelessly flirt with any and all of
these disgraceful drones. It's no use to warn them that the subject in
question is evidently a drug trafficker, that we comment on the
switchblade scar crossing his face from left to right, or that we
offer her a bottle of perfume to drown out the zoo-like stench he's
exuding. Even in this extreme situation, what is expected of a man, as
a proud heir to an illustrious tradition of clumsy oafs dating back to
the Precambrian period, is that he compulsively persist in his
pathetic display of arrhythmic convulsions.

Apparently, to please a woman we must allow ourselves to be blinded,
deafened, beaten, fleeced, and even humiliated in public in an absurd
competition with beings of dubious character. Do they think we lack all
self-respect? Have we given them reasons to think we are capable of any
baseness just to win them over? I'm afraid it looks like we did.
