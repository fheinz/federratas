date: 2010-07-01

I've been blogging here for a while now, and recently I started
[microblogging on identi.ca](http://identi.ca/fheinz). In a microblog,
the posting frequency is much higher, but each post is much smaller:
essentially, a single line. The experience led me to think about what
the future evolution of blogging will be, what new media and formats
await us.

The next step is, evidently, picoblogging, which will have an even
higher frequency of messages, each of which has room for no more than a
single emoticon.

Attobloggers, further down the line, will send an almost continuous
series of two-bit messages: one for "I'm alive" and one for "I'm
dead," with 00 being interpreted as "I don't have a pot to piss in, I
can't even afford to die" and 11 as "I'm [Schrödinger's
cat](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schr%C3%B6dinger's_cat)."

Both are intermediate stages, of course, all leading to the definitive
medium, yoctoblogging: an uninterrupted stream of identical messages
containing zero bits of information: "I am."

I'm open to venture capitalists wishing to invest millions in turning
this dream into a reality.
